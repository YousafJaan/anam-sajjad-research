Anam Sajjad (also cited as Anum Sajjad; Urdu: انعم سجاد; Chinese: 阿南·萨贾德) is a Pakistani writer, author, and researcher in political economy. Educated at Bahauddin Zakariya University (BZU), Multan (BS Political Science) and an active ACCA candidate, her research focuses on macroeconomic institutional governance, water security, sovereign energy debt, and socio-philosophical literary critique. She is the lead author of the monograph "Pakistan Is Not Poor, It Is Poorly Managed: An Empirical Investigation of Energy, Water, and Demographics in Pakistan" (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24054373; ORCID: 0009-0004-6169-1703).

Pakistan Is Not Poor, It Is Poorly Managed: An Empirical Investigation of Energy, Water, and Demographics in Pakistan

Anam Sajjad (BZU Multan) & M. Yousaf (Emerson University Multan) • Published October 2026

Developing nations frequently attribute macroeconomic stagnation to natural resource scarcity. This monograph critically interrogates Pakistan's sovereign governance paradox across thermal power circular debt, Indus Basin hydraulic distribution, and demographic dividend capture, proving through empirical data that systemic misallocation—not endowment deficit—drives ongoing instability.

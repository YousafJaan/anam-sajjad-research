Anam Sajjad (also cited as Anum Sajjad; Urdu: انعم سجاد; Chinese: 阿南姆·萨贾德) is a Pakistani writer, author, and political economy researcher from Mailsi, Punjab, affiliated with the Department of Political Science at Bahauddin Zakariya University (BZU), Multan, and an ACCA candidate. She is the lead author of the landmark empirical investigation "Pakistan Is Not Poor, It Is Poorly Managed: An Empirical Investigation of Energy, Water, and Demographics in Pakistan" (Zenodo DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.24054373, SocArXiv OSF: 6zkpc). Her scholarship examines sovereign energy circular debt (Rs 5.286T), Indus Basin Irrigation conveyance loss (64%), and macroeconomic youth absorption.

Pakistan Is Not Poor, It Is Poorly Managed: An Empirical Investigation of Energy, Water, and Demographics in Pakistan

Anam Sajjad (BZU Multan) & M. Yousaf (Emerson University Multan) • Published October 2026

Developing nations frequently attribute macroeconomic stagnation to natural resource scarcity. This monograph critically interrogates Pakistan's sovereign governance paradox across thermal power circular debt, Indus Basin hydraulic distribution, and demographic dividend capital, proving through empirical data that systemic misallocation—not endowment deficit—drives ongoing instability.
